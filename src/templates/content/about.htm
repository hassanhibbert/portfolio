About

From sketchbooks to schemas.

My path into software started with art: drawing and painting, then graphic design, then designing and building websites. Along the way I found myself drawn less to the visual choices, which often come down to taste, and more to the engineering underneath them.

Code offered the same room for creativity, with clearer measures of quality: correctness, performance, and maintainability. I moved into frontend development full time, and early on wrote and open-sourced small UI plugins for tooltips, input masks, and form validation. Over the years my work has followed the data further down the stack.

A lot of what I build now starts with a problem in my own life: a workout app for my training, a tracker for dialing in my cappuccinos, and, currently, an automatic plant-watering system. I build the first version for myself, then take it the rest of the way, with a real API, authentication, tests, and deployment, as if a team depended on it.

Outside of work: boxing and kickboxing, and keeping those plants alive.
