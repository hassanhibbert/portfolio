About

From sketchbooks to schemas.

My path into software started with art: drawing and painting, then graphic design, then designing and building websites. Along the way I found myself drawn less to the visual choices, which often come down to taste, and more to the engineering underneath them.

Code offered the same room for creativity, with clearer measures of quality: correctness, performance, and maintainability. I moved into frontend development full time, and over the years my work has followed the data further down the stack.

Early on, I wrote and open-sourced small UI plugins for tooltips, input masks, and form validation. Building tools for other developers has been a favorite kind of work ever since.

My design background still shows up in component libraries and UI work. I take ownership of what I work on: when a project crosses my desk, it should leave in better shape than it arrived.

Outside of work: boxing and kickboxing, a slowly recovering collection of indoor plants, and chasing a café-quality cappuccino at home.
